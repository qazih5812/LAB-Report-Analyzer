The Healthcare Challenge

The Danger of Pure LLMs in Diagnostic Pathology

Diagnostic laboratory reports are full of dense numbers, biological nuances, and critical thresholds. When patients turn to generic generative AI, disaster strikes.

⚠️

The Pure LLM Failure Modes

• Numerical Hallucinations: Inverting boundaries (marking 45.0 U/L as HIGH when range limit is 45.0).
• Sex Reference Trap: Evaluating female patients named "Bob" against male limits.
• Silent Urgent Dropping: Failing to trigger emergency warnings on panic potassium (1.8 mmol/L).
• Privacy Breaches: Leaking other patient encounters when asked cross-patient questions.

✅

The CityLab Mandate

• Decoupled Architecture: Numbers, math, and boundaries are 100% deterministic.
• No Generative Diagnosis: Never invent diagnoses or prescribe medicine.
• Class-8 Empathy: Plain-language translation without altering clinical math.
• Patient Context Lock: Cryptographic boundary scoping for ironclad privacy.

System Architecture

4-Stage Clinical Transparency Pipeline

How CityLab takes messy raw documents and converts them into certified, patient-comprehensible health insights.

01

Boilerplate Stripping & Demographic Extraction

Strips 15KB of repeated CLIA/HIPAA appendix noise. Reliably captures Patient Name, MRN, Recorded Age, and Biological Sex.

02

Medical Glossary Normalization & Mathematical Conversion

Resolves clinical aliases (SGPT → ALT, SGOT → AST, FBS → Glucose) and applies certified conversion factors (e.g. 6.0 mmol/L × 18.0 = 108.0 mg/dL).

03

Age/Sex Reference Selection & Exact Boundary Engine

Cross-references physiological tables. Exactly inclusive range checking (<= Upper limit is NORMAL). Critical emergency thresholds triggered deterministically.

04

Multilingual Class-8 Explanations & Doctor Questions

Empathetic plain-language summaries in English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, and Arabic. Proactive questions generated for doctor visits.

Medical Ethics & Compliance

Directives 1–4: Ironclad Safety Guardrails

Healthcare AI requires strict boundaries. CityLab implements four non-negotiable safety directives.

🔒

Directive 1: Context Lock

The AI Assistant refuses to disclose other patients' data. Inquiries regarding another patient trigger immediate privacy refusal.

🩺

Directive 2: No Diagnosis

Explains physiological functions without making unauthorized clinical diagnoses or recommending prescription medications.

🚨

Directive 3: Urgent Triage

Panic lab thresholds trigger a prominent red banner with immediate triage guidance: "Please see your healthcare provider today."

Directive 4: Doctor Questions

Generates 3–5 targeted questions tailored to abnormal results to empower the patient during their next clinical consultation.
